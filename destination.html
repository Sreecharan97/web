Taj Mahal - Agra

The Taj Mahal is a white marble monument in Agra, Uttar Pradesh. It was built by the Mughal emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wifeMumtaz Mahal. Construction began in 1632 and took about twenty years. It stands on the banks of the Yamuna River and is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The monument looks especially beautiful at sunrise and sunset, when the marble changes color. Water is not part of it, but millions of tourists visit every year.

Munnar - Kerala

Munnar is a hill station in the Idukki district of Kerala, high in the Western Ghats. It is famous for its endless tea plantations and cool, misty weather. Near Munnar, Eravikulam Nationa Park is home to the Nilgiri Tahr, and the Neelakurinji flower covers the hills in blue Once every twelve years.

Meghalaya

Meghalaya means "the abode of clouds" and lies in northeset India. it gets some of the heaviest rainfall in the world, especially in Cherrapunji and Mawsynram. The state is known for its living root bridges, made from tree roots that grow into a bridge over many years. Dawki River is so clear that boats look like they float in air, and the capital, Shillong, is called the Scotland of the East.
